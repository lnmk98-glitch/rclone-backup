Privacy Policy

rclone Backup is a private utility used only by the application owner.

Google Drive Data

The application accesses Google Drive only to upload, read, manage, and delete backup files created by the owner's rclone configuration.

Data Collection

No Google user data is collected, sold, shared, or transferred to third parties by this website.

Data Storage

Backup data is transferred directly between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive account.

Third Parties

This application is not provided to third-party users.
